# 02 — Problem and Market Context

## Structural Problem

Each enterprise platform stores only its local truth:
- cloud logs,
- IAM events,
- CI/CD and repository activity,
- ticketing systems,
- collaboration channels,
- AI model and agent usage.

No platform provides complete cross-system business context for operational decisions.
No platform shows the living ROI of a decision after it moves from business approval to code, infrastructure and AI consumption.

The core market gap is not cost visibility alone. It is **decision value visibility**:

- AI spend grows faster than internal controls.
- Cost ownership is unclear after deployment.
- Cloud and AI resources remain active after projects lose value.
- Companies measure cost, but rarely connect it to business value.
- Jira, GitHub, AWS and AI providers each know only one part of the story.

## Consequences

- Slow investigations (hours/days)
- Diffuse accountability
- Hidden AI and cloud costs
- Lost decision-level ROI visibility
- Incomplete end-to-end risk visibility
- Expensive and manual compliance work

## Market Timing

Multi-cloud complexity and AI adoption are growing faster than governance capabilities.  
Continuous compliance and audit automation are becoming strategic requirements.

## Opportunity

Build a Decision ROI Platform that converts technical events into explainable, measurable and optimizable business decisions.

The MVP opportunity is to prove a narrow AI/cloud spend recovery wedge with one first action:

1. Downgrade or change AI model.

After this wedge is repeatable, IMPERATOR can expand into unused AI agent removal, underutilized AWS resource detection tied to the same Decision ROI Case, negative-ROI feature analysis and duplicated service or agent consolidation.
