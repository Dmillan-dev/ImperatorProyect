Status: **PREPARATION ONLY / SYNTHETIC DATA / NO CUSTOMER CONNECTION**

## 1. Decision This Document Supports

This demonstration answers one commercial question:

> Does the product already make one operational recovery Decision easier to
> trust, govern and value for a real company?

It does not ask whether IMPERATOR has enough features. It tests whether a CTO,
Platform owner and Finance reviewer understand and trust one complete
Decision ROI Case.

## 9. Five Buyer Validation Questions

Ask these verbatim after the walkthrough:

1. How do you reconstruct this kind of Decision today, and how long does it
   take across Platform, Finance and leadership?
2. Which part of the Evidence-to-Recommendation chain would you need to trust
   before approving an action?
3. Is one decision-level recovery estimate more actionable than your current
   cloud or AI cost dashboard? Why?
4. Who would own the first real case, who would approve it and who would
   validate the result?
5. What would have to be true for you to run a bounded 30-day pilot?

Do not lead the buyer toward a positive answer. Capture exact language and the
missing proof they name.

## 10. Value Validation Scorecard

| Signal | Measurement | PASS threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Problem recognition | Buyer confirms a recurring cross-system reconstruction problem | Explicit example from the buyer |
| Comprehension | Time until sponsor can restate Decision, action and value | 15 minutes or less |
| Evidence trust | Average buyer rating from 1-5 | At least 4, with no unresolved critical source gap |
| Recommendation actionability | Sponsor says the action could enter review | Yes, subject to named checks |
| Economic credibility | Finance accepts the calculation method and can name its own source data | Yes |
| Ownership | Business, technical, approver and Finance roles can be named | All four |
| Data feasibility | Required normalized facts can be supplied without Restricted data | Yes |
| Pilot intent | Sponsor agrees to a concrete next step and date | Yes |
| Willingness to pay | Buyer accepts discussing a paid pilot or explicit budget test | Positive or testable, never inferred |

### Outcome

| Result | Rule |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN PARTNER CANDIDATE` | Every mandatory qualification passes and at least seven scorecard signals pass, including problem, evidence trust, economic credibility and pilot intent |
| `DISCOVERY CONTINUE` | Problem exists but ownership, data or economic proof has one bounded gap |
| `NO-GO` | No recurring problem, no sponsor, no measurable baseline, prohibited data requirement or demand outside the MVP boundary |
