# 04 — Value Proposition

## Core Value Proposition

IMPERATOR unifies cross-platform operational context and transforms it into traceability, risk intelligence, and cost optimization.

## Painkillers (Prioritized by Buying Urgency)

### 1) Cross-platform Decision Traceability (Entry Wedge)
Problem: “We don’t know who made a decision and under what context.”

Outcomes:
- full traceability across person/agent/policy/cost/outcome/owner
- faster investigations
- stronger accountability

### 2) Operational Risk Intelligence
Problem: “We don’t know where operational risk is.”

Outcomes:
- excessive access detection
- risky agent behavior patterns
- ownerless resources
- policy non-compliance visibility

### 3) AI Governance & Cost Intelligence
Problem: “We don’t know real AI usage and cost.”

Outcomes:
- model usage visibility by team/use case
- cost attribution
- governance and value tracking

## Recommended North-Star KPI

**Reduce operational investigation time from hours/days to minutes.**
