# 09 — Business Model Hypotheses

## Initial Monetization Hypotheses

Possible pricing axes:
1. number of connected systems,
2. volume of processed decisions/events,
3. advanced intelligence modules,
4. compliance/audit package tier.

## Defensible Value Unit

Value is created through:
- investigation MTTR reduction,
- hidden-cost reduction,
- audit-effort reduction.

## Willingness-to-Pay Hypothesis

Strongest in organizations with:
- real multi-cloud complexity,
- compliance pressure,
- rapidly growing AI usage.
