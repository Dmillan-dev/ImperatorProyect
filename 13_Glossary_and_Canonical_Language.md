- Operational Intelligence Layer

- “traditional observability” as primary definition
- “Decision Intelligence” as core category label

1. “We don’t replace enterprise systems; we connect them with business context.”
2. “From isolated events to accountable decisions.”
3. “One operational truth across cloud, SaaS, and AI.”
