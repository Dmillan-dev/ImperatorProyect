# 03 — ICP and Buyer Personas

## Primary ICP (Winner Focus)

**Enterprise SaaS Multi-cloud** with:
- 100–500 employees
- 50+ SaaS applications
- More than one cloud provider
- Active AI usage (models and/or agents)
- Platform Engineering team
- DevOps team
- Security team

## Recommended Initial Subsegment (v1)

**B2B SaaS under compliance pressure (SOC2/ISO27001) with high operational complexity.**

## Buying Map

### Primary Operational Buyer
- Platform Engineering Manager

### Internal Champion
- Staff Engineer (Platform / Infrastructure)

### Technical Buyer
- CTO

### Economic Buyer
- CIO or VP Engineering

## Strong Pain Signals

- “We can’t quickly reconstruct who decided what.”
- “AI and cloud costs are not properly attributable.”
- “Audits are too manual and slow.”
